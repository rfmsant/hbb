Once you live in Portugal, Portuguese inheritance law may apply to your estate, including assets abroad.

## Forced heirship

Portuguese law reserves part of the estate for spouses, children and, in some cases, parents. You cannot freely leave everything to whoever you choose.

## Choosing your home country's law

Under the EU Succession Regulation, you can choose the law of your nationality in your will. For many clients, this keeps their existing plans intact.

## A will that works here

A Portuguese will, or a foreign will that clearly makes that choice, avoids uncertainty for your family later.
