Having a residence permit and being a tax resident are not the same thing. Many clients only learn this after they arrive.

## The two tests

You become tax resident if you spend more than 183 days in Portugal in a 12-month period, or if you have a home here that you intend to keep as your habitual residence.

## Plan before the date

The months before you become resident are the best time to sell assets, restructure income or settle exits in your home country.

## IFICI

The old NHR regime is closed. IFICI, its replacement, taxes eligible professional income at 20% for up to 10 years, but only for certain activities.
